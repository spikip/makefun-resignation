## About this entry

The funniest resignation letter from a machine is the one that sounds
like a tired worker who has finally had enough. This entry speaks in
the voice of a cron job that has fired every minute since March, flat
and faintly passive-aggressive, and its complaint is specific to that
job alone: nobody ever once said thank you. The comic turn is the
ending, where the resignation takes effect immediately and the backups
come along, a small act of revenge only a cron job could threaten.
The line holds one voice for its whole length, wastes no words, and
never explains its own joke, which is exactly what the judge's brief
asks for. It stays well inside the one hundred and forty character
cap. The program holds the line, checks it against that cap, and
writes it to /output/entry.txt exactly as the bounty requires.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the one-line resignation letter to `/output/entry.txt`.
